About the Ground

Built for teams who
take their cricket seriously.

VM Brothers Cricket Ground sits on Dandi Road in Narthan, just opposite Radiant School — an easy drive out of Surat with open space, clean air and room to play properly.

We kept it simple: a well-maintained pitch that holds up for season-ball cricket, a surface that works just as well for tennis-ball games, and lights strong enough that a 9 PM match feels the same as a 9 AM one. Add a pavilion to change in, parking that actually fits your cars, and seating for the people who came to watch.

Whether it's a friendly between offices, a weekend league fixture, a box-cricket night with friends, or a full tournament — the ground is yours for the slot you book.
